# Henar — update, 16–18 September 2026

Measured against the running product, not estimated. 351 tests → **450 (449 passing), lint and typecheck clean.**

## Router: from a Jupiter front end to a meta aggregator
- Measured the gap: Henar lost a median **13 bps at $1,000, 18 bps at $10,000** to Jupiter.
- **Found the unexplained half.** Jupiter answers at two endpoints; we asked one. `/swap/v2/order` reaches its RFQ network (262 bps on AMC), but on liquid names `/swap/v1/quote` came back ~10 bps better — so we were reselling Jupiter *below* Jupiter's own price, then charging 10 bps on top.
- **Racing both endpoints** took the residual beyond our own fee from **-10.0 bps to +0.7**. User-facing gap halved: **-10.0 / -9.7 bps**, now exactly the fee and nothing accidental.
- **Splits made real:** leg cap 2 → 3, refinement generalised. Splits now beat the best single pool **every time, median +3 bps, up to +21**. Real three-venue routes: SPCX = Raydium 55% / Meteora 26% / Orca 19%.
- **Two venues we were blind to.** Meteora had *zero* public-equity pools — its discovery script had never run (SDK failure). Fixed: 291 pairs found, **50 above the liquidity floor** (Micron $4.2M, SKHY $2.4M). Raydium CPMM was disabled for lacking an adapter it already had.
- Registry **1,200 → 1,491 pools, 321 → 372 enabled**, all verified on chain.
- **Never quote a price we can't fill.** OpenOcean and Titan have no builder, so when they won, users saw a number the order couldn't produce — **4 of 23 quotes, once by 11.6 bps**. The headline is now the best *fillable* quote; benchmarks stay visible, labelled Reference.

## DBC: refocused on a gap in Henar itself
- **Removed the config wizard from the product.** A 21-field launch tool is an issuer's job in an investor's navigation. The route survives behind its flag as an operator tool, deploy path intact.
- Measured the marketplace instead: **Henar lists 1,339 companies and can trade 94.** 2,105 of 2,212 verified representations have no pool — not a listing problem but a liquidity one, which is exactly what a bonding curve bootstraps.
- **So on a company we can't trade, the company page now proposes the exact market that would open it** — sized from the live reference price, nothing to fill in: *ABBV / USDC, opens at $262 per token, graduates once it raises $25,000, then migrates into Meteora DAMM v2.*
- It proposes; it does not yet launch. Creating the market signs from an operator wallet through the existing Studio deploy path, which is not wired to this card.
- Why it belongs here: a curve pairing a verified representation with USDC is router-eligible while it bonds, so a market opened this way is quotable by our own router from the moment it exists.
