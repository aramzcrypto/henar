# Henar — what changed, 16–18 September 2026

Every number here was measured against the running product, not estimated.
Baseline at the start: 351 tests, 350 passing. Now: **450 tests, 449 passing,
1 intentionally skipped, lint and typecheck clean.**

---

## Router: from a Jupiter front end to a real meta aggregator

**Measured the gap first.** Against Jupiter's own quote, same pair and size,
Henar was losing a median 13 bps at $1,000 and 18 bps at $10,000.

- **Found the unexplained half.** Jupiter answers the same question at two
  endpoints and Henar only asked one. `/swap/v2/order` reaches JupiterZ, its
  RFQ network, worth 262 bps on AMC and 102 on IBM. But on liquid names
  `/swap/v1/quote` came back ~10 bps better, in 7 of 14 pairs. Henar was
  reselling Jupiter *below* Jupiter's own price and charging 10 bps on top.
- **Fixed it by racing both endpoints.** The residual beyond Henar's own fee
  went from **-10.0 bps to +0.7 bps**, with 0 of 12 pairs worse than -2 bps.
- **User-facing gap halved**: $1,000 from -13.0 to **-10.0 bps**; $10,000 from
  -18.2 to **-9.7 bps**. The gap is now exactly the fee and nothing accidental.
- Jupiter's provider fee was recorded as `0` without ever reading the field.
  It is read now.

**Split routing made real.**

- Leg cap raised from two to three, and refinement generalised: it used to
  refine only the first pair's boundary, leaving every other boundary on a
  coarse grid.
- **Result: splits beat the best single pool every time, median +3 bps, up to
  +21.** Genuine three-venue routes now exist — SPCX at $10,000 goes Raydium
  54.66%, Meteora 26.28%, Orca 19.06%.

**Two venues Henar was blind to.**

- **Meteora had no public-equity pools at all** — not disabled, absent. The
  discovery script had never run: the DLMM SDK fails under plain `tsx`. Fixed,
  it read 158,700 on-chain accounts and found 291 pairs, **50 above the
  liquidity floor**, including Micron at $4.2M and SKHY at $2.4M. 41
  representations gained a venue.
- **Raydium CPMM was disabled for lacking an adapter it already had.**
- Registry: **1,200 → 1,491 pools, 321 → 372 enabled, nothing lost, every
  enabled pool verified on chain.**

**Two latent bugs found on the way.**

- `npm run router:pools:build` silently deleted every row it does not own —
  431 routing legs and all 182 private-market pools — because three scripts
  write that file and one overwrote the other two.
- The engine handed each adapter every pool of its venue, so the two Raydium
  adapters each received the other's pools and paid for the RPC reads to find
  out they could not quote them.

**Honesty fix: never quote a price Henar cannot fill.**

- The ticket ranked four sources purely on output, but OpenOcean and Titan have
  no builder anywhere in Henar. Whenever they won, the user was shown a number
  the order could never produce — **4 of 23 equity quotes, once by 11.6 bps.**
- The headline is now the best *fillable* quote. Benchmark sources stay visible,
  labelled Reference.

**Where the remaining gap is.** Henar beats its own Jupiter quote in **1 of 28
observations (4%), by 6 bps**. Jupiter's edge comes from permissioned market
makers (HumidiFi, ZeroFi, BisonFi, GoonFi, Kipseli, Archer). Correction on
record: these are *ordinary on-chain programs*, not off-chain RFQ — reachable
by pricing them through simulation rather than reimplementing their curves.

## Pricing

- A performance fee was proposed, then **measured and withdrawn**: at 4%
  outperformance it would earn 0.04 bps per trade. That is not a fee model.
- Recommendation on file: keep 10 bps for retail size, taper to 5 bps above
  $5,000 and 3 bps above $25,000. Price impact in this book runs 30–50 bps on
  $1,000, so the fee is a fifth of the spread a user already pays — but a flat
  rate on notional is indefensible at size, which is exactly where Henar adds
  nothing to the price.

## Pyth

- Key live in production. **TSLA and QQQ underlying feeds readable, all four
  channels, full history; every tokenized feed not entitled.**
- **Fixed a probe bug that under-reported the key**: the entitlement sample was
  taken off the front of an 897-name list and missed TSLA entirely, then tested
  channels and history against a feed it could not read. Reported NOT_ENTITLED
  for a working key.
- **Market data page rebuilt.** It opened on coverage statistics — an operations
  dashboard that showed no price and advertised how little the key reads. It now
  leads with live prices, session, publisher count, confidence and age, with
  every tokenized claim beside its underlying. Coverage moved underneath.
- The two halves stream separately: the entitlement probe asks about 119 feeds
  and was blocking the prices for ~49 seconds.
- The trade ticket used to open on a company outside the entitlement, so the
  first thing anyone saw was a fair-value panel reporting no reference.

## Product surfaces

**Landing page**

- Removed the Simple/Pro toggle. There is no Pro terminal and one is not being
  built; it promised a product that does not exist in the most prominent slot
  of the Trade section. Replaced with what Henar actually built — the venues it
  routes across, three-venue splits, and simulation before signing.
- Added a **Built on** section naming Pyth, PreStocks, Tessera and Meteora, each
  with one verifiable detail and a link to the surface that proves it.
- Hero now full-bleed; it used to end in two visible vertical seams.
- Ambient light replaced a flat near-black page. Two failed attempts recorded:
  a coloured wash (mud, buried the globe) and a ruled grid (the house style of
  every generated landing page).
- Section padding cut from 300px between bands to 224px — most of a laptop
  screen of nothing.
- Globe now sized against the hero's height, so it neither clips on a 14" screen
  nor strands at the bottom of a 27" one.

**Light mode**

- Not a toggle — a design-system migration. The product had **925 colour
  literals across 539 unique values**, only a third going through tokens.
- Colours given *roles* by the property they sit on (a hex in `background` is a
  surface; the same hex in `color` is text), then mapped onto ordered ramps.
  **913 literals migrated.**
- Light is a peer, not an inversion: greys re-picked for a light ground, state
  colours re-picked at the same meaning, shadows and hairlines flipped outright.
- Hero globe uses cobe's inverted lighting; the ambient layer becomes shade;
  grain multiplies instead of overlaying.
- **Two latent bugs found**: two later `:root` blocks redeclared `--bg` and
  `--border` with fixed dark values, which would have pinned the product to dark
  whatever the theme asked; and three stylesheets were never migrated, leaving a
  dark Connect button with dark text on every page.
- **A real toggle bug**: switching to dark left surfaces on the old theme,
  because a transitioned property reading from `var()` does not reliably pick up
  a change to the custom property. Transitions are now suppressed across the flip.
- Contrast swept automatically: Markets went from 12 failures below the AA floor
  to zero; Portfolio and Docs pass clean.

## DBC — rebuilt around a gap in Henar itself

- **Studio was removed from the product.** It is an issuer tool in an investor's
  navigation, and no amount of tidying the form fixed that. The route survives
  behind its flag as an operator tool.
- **Measured the marketplace and found the real gap: Henar lists 1,339 companies
  and can trade 94.** 2,105 of 2,212 verified representations have no enabled
  pool anywhere. Not a listing problem — the mints are verified on chain. A
  liquidity problem.
- **So: on a company Henar cannot trade, it now offers to open a market.** Two
  clicks, nothing to fill in. AbbVie reads: ABBV / USDC, opens at $262 per token,
  graduates once it raises $25,000, then migrates into Meteora DAMM v2.
- This is part of the marketplace, not a launchpad beside it: a curve pairing a
  verified representation with USDC is router-eligible while it bonds, so the
  market is quotable by Henar's own router from the moment it exists.
