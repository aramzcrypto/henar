## Router: from Jupiter front end to meta aggregator
- We lost a median **13 bps at $1,000, 18 bps at $10,000** to Jupiter. It answers at two endpoints; we asked one. `/swap/v2/order` reaches its RFQ network (262 bps on AMC), but on liquid names `/swap/v1/quote` came back ~10 bps better — so we resold Jupiter *below* its own price, then charged 10 bps on top.
- **Racing both endpoints** took the residual beyond our fee from **-10.0 bps to +0.7**, halving the gap to **-10.0 / -9.7 bps** — now exactly the fee.
- **Splits made real:** leg cap 2 → 3, refinement generalised. They beat the best single pool **every time, median +3 bps** — SPCX routes Raydium 55% / Meteora 26% / Orca 19%.
- **Two venues we were blind to.** Meteora had *zero* public-equity pools; its discovery script had never run. Fixed: 291 pairs, **50 above the floor**. Raydium CPMM was disabled for lacking an adapter it already had.
- Registry **1,200 → 1,491 pools, 321 → 372 enabled**, all verified on chain.
- **Never quote a price we can't fill.** OpenOcean and Titan have no builder, so when they won users saw a number the order couldn't produce — **4 of 23 quotes, once by 11.6 bps**.
